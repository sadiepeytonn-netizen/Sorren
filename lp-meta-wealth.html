COMMON QUESTIONS

Straight answers.

Is this an investment?

No. Life insurance is not a security, bank deposit or investment. Some policies build cash value, but its growth is not guaranteed.

Can I really access the cash value?

Depending on the policy, you may be able to borrow against it or withdraw from it. Doing so reduces the death benefit and available cash value and may have tax consequences.

Do I have to buy something?

No. The guide and the call are free, with no obligation to purchase a policy.

Is this tax or financial advice?

No. This is educational. Talk with your tax or financial professional about your own situation.
